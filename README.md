# Awesome-Marketing-Automation

## Top Marketing Automation Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Email Journeys, Lead Nurturing, Campaign Orchestration, Segmentation, CRM Integration & Multichannel Engagement*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Marketing Automation**. These systems orchestrate email and multichannel campaigns, nurture leads, score behavior, personalize content, and connect marketing with CRM and product data.



**Examples** include Marketo, HubSpot Marketing Hub, ActiveCampaign, Mailchimp, Klaviyo, Customer.io, Pardot, Brevo, Iterable, and Ortto (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Marketing automation has a strong open-source flagship in **Mautic**. Additional open tools such as **Listmonk** (high-volume email), developer-first journey engines, and self-hosted newsletter platforms give teams real alternatives for ownership and cost control. Commercial platforms still lead in polished UX, deep CRM ecosystems, and deliverability infrastructure. This section is heavily expanded.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Marketo Engage (Adobe)](https://business.adobe.com/products/marketo.html)**  

  Enterprise B2B marketing automation platform for complex lead nurturing, account-based marketing, and deep integration with Adobe Experience Cloud.



- **[HubSpot Marketing Hub](https://www.hubspot.com/products/marketing)**  

  All-in-one marketing automation platform with strong CRM integration, visual workflows, email, landing pages, and inbound tools for growing companies.



- **[ActiveCampaign](https://www.activecampaign.com/)**  

  Marketing automation and CRM platform popular with SMBs for email automation, lead scoring, and customer experience workflows.



- **[Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/)**  

  Widely used email marketing and automation platform with audience management, journeys, and e-commerce integrations.



- **[Klaviyo](https://www.klaviyo.com/)**  

  Data-driven marketing automation platform especially strong for e-commerce brands, with deep customer profiling and multichannel campaigns.



- **[Customer.io](https://customer.io/)**  

  Developer-friendly messaging and automation platform focused on behavioral emails, push, and in-app messages triggered by product events.



- **[Pardot (Salesforce Marketing Cloud Account Engagement)](https://www.salesforce.com/products/marketing-cloud/)**  

  B2B marketing automation tightly integrated with Salesforce CRM for lead nurturing, scoring, and sales alignment.



- **[Brevo (formerly Sendinblue)](https://www.brevo.com/)**  

  Multichannel marketing platform offering email, SMS, chat, and automation for SMBs and mid-market teams.



- **[Iterable](https://iterable.com/)**  

  Cross-channel marketing and customer engagement platform designed for personalized, event-driven campaigns at scale.



- **[Ortto (formerly Autopilot)](https://ortto.com/)**  

  Customer journey and marketing automation platform combining campaigns, analytics, and CDP-style audience capabilities.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Mautic](https://github.com/mautic/mautic)**  

  The world’s largest open-source marketing automation project—campaigns, email, forms, landing pages, lead management, segmentation, and a strong self-hosted community.



- **[Listmonk](https://github.com/knadh/listmonk)**  

  High-performance, self-hosted newsletter and mailing-list manager (Go + Postgres) optimized for large subscriber lists and transactional/bulk email.



- **[Dittofeed and developer-first journey engines](https://github.com/)**  

  Open-source, developer-oriented platforms for building multichannel customer journeys and messaging infrastructure.



- **[Postal](https://github.com/postalserver/postal)**  

  Open-source mail delivery platform for sending and tracking application and marketing email at scale.



- **[CiviCRM](https://github.com/civicrm/civicrm-core)**  

  Open-source CRM with strong email and campaign capabilities, widely used by nonprofits and associations.



- **[phpList](https://github.com/phpList/phplist3)**  

  Long-standing open-source newsletter and email campaign manager.



- **[Novu](https://github.com/novuhq/novu)**  

  Open-source notification infrastructure for in-app, email, SMS, and push—useful as a delivery layer under custom automation.



- **[N8N / open workflow automation for marketing](https://github.com/n8n-io/n8n)**  

  Open-source workflow tool frequently used to orchestrate marketing integrations, lead routing, and custom journeys.



- **[Self-hosted email and preference-center open tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Components for subscription management, preference centers, and compliant unsubscribe handling.



- **[Segment and reverse-ETL open alternatives](https://github.com/)**  

  Open data pipelines that feed product and customer events into marketing automation systems.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Deploying **Mautic** as the primary open-source marketing automation suite for full campaign and lead management.

- Using **Listmonk** when high-volume newsletters and simple automation are the main need.

- Combining open messaging infrastructure (Postal, Novu) with custom journey logic for product-led teams.

- Accepting that enterprise deliverability, advanced ABM, deep native CRM suites, and polished visual builders still favor commercial platforms (Marketo, HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, Klaviyo, Pardot, Iterable, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership, privacy, cost control, and freedom from per-contact pricing.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Self-host Mautic or Listmonk → connect CRM and product events → design journeys and segments → send via a reputable ESP or self-hosted MTA → measure engagement and feed results back into the CRM. Suitable for privacy-conscious organizations and teams with ops capacity. Many growth and marketing teams still choose commercial platforms for speed, support, and ecosystem integrations.
